From idea to impact.

The programme can evolve from a challenge into a longer support pathway.

01

Challenge

Participants identify a real problem and submit a solution concept.

02

Selection

Shortlisted participants are assessed on problem clarity, creativity, feasibility and potential value.

03

Build Sprint

Participants receive workshops, mentorship and practical support to develop a prototype or minimum viable solution.

04

Market Test

Participants test their solution with real users, gather feedback and refine their offer.

05

Demo Day

Finalists present the solution, evidence of progress and a realistic plan for the next stage.

06

Post-Challenge Growth

Selected ventures can receive continued mentorship, introductions, visibility and access to relevant opportunities.

MAIDEN CONCEPT

Scholarship prize + something more valuable: a pathway to build.

The prize is the spark. The deeper objective is to help participants develop solutions that can create value beyond the competition.
